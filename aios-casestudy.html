01 · Problem

The Research
Challenge

As mobile operating systems rapidly evolve, AIOS faces a critical gap in understanding how users in high-growth African markets interact with key OS modules — particularly Global Search and Smart Suggestions. Preparing for long-term product planning requires deep, locally grounded insight.


Kenya's mobile users increasingly rely on a hybrid ecosystem of native OS features and third-party AI tools — ChatGPT, Gemini, Ella, Deepseek — to meet daily academic, professional, and personal needs. Yet OS-level features remain largely misaligned with local behaviors, cultural contexts, and linguistic realities.


The core challenge: identify, validate, and prioritize localization opportunities for AIOS's roadmap that are both globally competitive and locally relevant — grounded in authentic Kenyan user behaviors, not assumptions.

OS Modules Under-Optimized for Local Behavior
Global Search and Smart Suggestions features lack alignment with how Kenyan users actually navigate information — mixing English, Swahili, and Sheng in daily searches.
Third-Party AI Tools Dominating Native OS
Users rely heavily on ChatGPT, Gemini, Ella, and Deepseek for tasks the OS should handle natively — a clear signal of unmet OS-level demand.
Voice & Translation Features Fail Local Languages
Voice typing and translation tools perform poorly with "Swanglish," Sheng, and dialectal Swahili — creating daily friction for millions of Kenyan users.
Notification Overload & Poor Contextual Intelligence
Users face notification fatigue from social media and promotional spam, with 65% finding existing management tools inadequate. AI-based sorting is desired but absent.
Fragmented Sports & News Consumption
Fans jump between multiple apps for scores, streaming, and betting — no single platform delivers a comprehensive local + international sports experience.

03 · Findings

Key Research
Findings

AI Cognition
5
AI Tools in Daily Use

ChatGPT dominates, followed by Ella (bundled), Gemini, Deepseek, and Perplexity. Users learned about AI primarily through social media, peers, and academic settings — with adoption accelerating from 2019 to 2024.

"Ella and Chatgpt are quite extensive in their responses since they leave me thinking. AI makes my work easier since these days I do not need to go to the library." — Peter, 20, Univ. Student
Usage Scenarios
5
Core AI Use Scenario Categories

Information seeking, content creation, problem-solving & learning, emotional companionship, and workflow efficiency. Laptops dominate for deep work (60%), phones for quick on-the-go lookups (40%).

"I mainly study on my laptop, at least 60% on laptop and 40% on phone." — Geff, 25, University Student & Freelancer
Voice Write Concept
#1
Rewrite & Translate Are Most Valued

The Rewrite sub-feature was most universally praised for tone adaptation and professional communication. Translate was seen as indispensable but only if it handles local Swahili dialects and Sheng — not formal "Kiswahili Sanifu."

"In English 'look out' is a warning, but its direct Swahili translation 'angalia nje' means something different: 'look outside'." — Elvis, Product Ops Manager
Zero Screen Concept
53%
Zero Screen is Preferred Display Location

Over half of participants preferred AI smart suggestions on the zero screen for glanceable, personalized daily info. Morning needs: weather + traffic + calendar. Afternoon: lunch spots + music. Evening: live football scores + news recaps.

"The calendar is a good option to assist in activity planning based on the timing." — Abigael, Operations Specialist
AI Global Search
#1
AI Search Ranked Most Useful Feature

AI Search within Global Search was the highest-rated sub-feature — consolidating multiple AI tools in one interface, enabling research without app-switching. Users want results shown in-interface, not browser redirects.

"It feels like Google but it's more fresh." — Elvis, 32, Product Operations Manager
Payments & Willingness
61%
Majority Willing to Pay for Premium AI

38.1% had previously paid for AI. 61% expressed future willingness, primarily for premium accuracy, specialized professional tools, and advanced media generation. Acceptable price range: KES 500–1,000/month.

"I would pay at least 1000 Ksh if the services are premium and fast." — Carolyne, 32, Content Consultant
Notifications
100%
All Users Prioritize Communication Alerts

WhatsApp and financial transaction notifications (M-Pesa) are universally critical. ~40% mute social media notifications. 65% find existing management tools inadequate. Users want AI-based sorting and contextual prioritization.

"I wish AI masters my habits and apps I consider important." — Geff, 25, University Student
Typing & Language
3
Languages Mixed in Every Message

Kenyan users seamlessly code-switch between English, Swahili, and Sheng within a single message. Standard keyboards (Gboard) fail this context — autocorrecting "leo" (Swahili for "today") to the Leo zodiac sign.

"The current voice typing features are not accurate and do not support our local language." — Elvis, 32, Product Ops Manager
Sports & Football
93%
Football is Kenya's #1 Unifying Sport

93% of Kenyans follow football. Users jump between Flashscore, SuperSport, and betting apps (SportPesa, Betika) for a fragmented experience. Live score updates are the most critical evening OS notification desired.

"Thinks Tecno should come up with a dedicated sports app to give sports news." — Nicholas, 25, Psychologist

"AI cognition among Kenyan users reflects deep recognition of AI's practical benefits, coupled with caution about privacy, job security, and ethical use — a balance that signals real opportunity for OS-level AI that is contextually intelligent, locally accurate, and trust-worthy."

05 · Impact

Strategic
Implications for AIOS

Localization Opportunities

Swahili, Sheng & Code-Switching Support

A keyboard and voice system that understands Kenyan linguistic reality — mixing English, Swahili, and Sheng within a single sentence — would solve a critical daily pain point that no current OEM adequately addresses for this market.

Informal Swahili Translation (Not "Sanifu")

Translation must prioritize Nairobi street Swahili and Sheng over formal Kiswahili Sanifu. Context-aware translation — understanding idioms, jokes, and cultural nuance — is the non-negotiable feature requirement for Kenyan users.

OS-Level AI Search as ChatGPT Alternative

Users want OS-native AI search that competes with ChatGPT and Gemini without app-switching — built directly into Global Search with in-interface responses, not browser redirects. This is a clear differentiation opportunity for AIOS.

Football & Sports as a Core OS Feature

With 93% of Kenyans following football, integrating real-time scores and local league coverage (KPL, Gor Mahia, AFC Leopards) directly into OS widgets and Zero Screen suggestions could create deep daily engagement and product loyalty.

Design & Roadmap Recommendations

Emoji & Search Sub-features: Deprioritize

Auto-emoji generation was almost universally rejected — users want manual control. Voice-to-browser search also scored last as it duplicates existing Google behavior without adding value. Resources should be reallocated to Rewrite and Translate.

Privacy-First Zero Screen Design

The Zero Screen concept faces significant privacy concerns. Displaying sensitive habits publicly was a major dislike. The recommendation: make suggestion content and display location fully customizable, with clear privacy controls visible to users.

AI Notification Sorting as Premium Differentiator

Intelligent notification management — AI that learns habits and filters promotional noise — represents an underserved, high-desire feature. With 65% of users unsatisfied with current tools, AI-based sorting is a strong retention and differentiation play.

Tiered Monetization: KES 500–1,000/Month Sweet Spot

61% of users are willing to pay, with price sensitivity clustering at KES 500 (lower tier) and KES 1,000 (premium tier). A freemium model with professional-grade AI features as paid upgrades aligns directly with the dominant user motivation.

Key Concerns to Address in Product Design
Privacy & Data Security

Users fear voice recordings going to the cloud without consent and worry about who has access to their data. "Who has access to my data? Would it be used against me?" — Vanessa, 21

Misinformation in Recommended Content

Clickbait and fake news showing up in Recommended features erodes trust. Users demand verified sources integrated into AI Search — especially for news and research content.

AI Accuracy & Reliability

AI tools that provide incorrect information destroy trust rapidly. Users cited ChatGPT's occasional misinformation and Gemini's over-reliance on search snippets as major pain points demanding active improvement.
