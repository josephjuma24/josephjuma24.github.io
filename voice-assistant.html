01 — Problem

What Problem Did This Research Address?

Voice assistants have become integral to daily life for millions of users worldwide. Yet in East African markets like Kenya, little data exists on how locally-deployed or consumer-grade assistants — including the homegrown Ella and Folax — actually perform against global incumbents like Google Assistant, Siri, and Bixby.

Without evidence-backed satisfaction data, product teams cannot prioritize improvements that matter most to Kenyan users. This study was commissioned to close that gap: to assess user satisfaction across ten functional areas, surface competitive advantages and weaknesses, and gather candid user expectations for future development.

Research Objectives

User demographics — age, gender, occupation, education
Usage frequency, time of day, scenarios and functions
Overall satisfaction and loyalty with voice assistants
Feature-level satisfaction: tone, accuracy, fluency, aesthetics
Brand perception and competitive advantages
User demands and expectations for future development

02 — Methods

How the Research Was Conducted

The research methodology consisted of offline in-depth interviews. Twelve Kenyan daily voice assistant users were recruited and interviewed individually, having consented to participation. The sampling was stratified by age, gender, and location to ensure a representative sample.

The questionnaire combined closed-ended and open-ended questions: closed questions captured demographics, usage patterns, and satisfaction ratings on a 1–10 scale; open questions collected qualitative insights on advantages, challenges, and improvement suggestions.

Two distinct comparison frameworks were used: a single satisfaction comparison rating across ten functional features, and a comprehensive satisfaction comparison across ten experiential dimensions, yielding a holistic picture of each assistant's strengths and weaknesses.

01

Entertainment: Ella Leads by a Wide Margin

91.7% of participants rated Ella higher than all competitors for playing music and video accurately and with a quick response time. Ella scored 8.3 average against 5.9 from competitors. Users noted Ella directly navigated to local Kenyan content including bongo music — a culturally specific edge competitors lacked.

02

Reminders: Competitors Dominate Unanimously

100% of all 12 users rated Google, Bixby & Siri higher than Ella for setting reminders, alarms, and calendar events. Ella consistently responded "I don't know how to add an event to your calendar," scoring just 3.6 vs. competitors' 8.6. This was the most decisive performance gap in the entire study.

03

Travel: Ella Excels at Navigation Detail

75% of users scored Ella higher for giving precise, detailed location and route information. Ella retrieved multi-modal directions from Nairobi to Mombasa covering road, rail and air — while Bixby misidentified Mombasa as Mumbai. Ella scored 8.0 vs. competitors' 6.8 average.

04

Small Talk: Ella Keeps Users Engaged

83.3% of users rated Ella higher for initiating relevant chats and conversational engagement. Ella scored 7.1 vs. competitors' 5.7. One user noted Ella responded warmly: "Hello my friend, what can I do for you?" — reflecting a friendlier interaction style than the more transactional Google or Siri.

05

Voice Search: Competitors Retrieve More Accurately

66.7% of users rated competitors higher for voice search accuracy. Users reported Ella retrieved incorrect information about Kenya's current president — a significant trust issue for a core feature. Competitors like Bixby narrated results with additional context while Ella's results were imprecise on current events.

06

Translation Tools: Ella Best for English-to-Swahili

75% rated Ella higher for translation, translating words directly without routing to external apps. However, all three (Ella, Bixby, Siri) failed to translate from Swahili to English — only Google managed this reverse direction. Ella scored 5.7 vs. competitors' 3.9 for translation overall.

03B — Comprehensive Satisfaction

Comprehensive Satisfaction Scores

Across ten experiential dimensions, Google Assistant led with a comprehensive average of 8.1 — above Bixby and Ella (both 7.6) and Siri (7.5). This reflects Google's strength in accuracy, feature richness, and interaction fluency.

Notably, Ella outperformed competitors in System Stability (8.6), Visual Aesthetics (7.9), and Interesting Perception (8.3) — areas tied to design polish and personality.

Ella's weaknesses concentrated in Feedback Accuracy Rate (6.9 vs. 7.5) and Overall Satisfaction (6.9 vs. 7.3) — driven largely by the reminders failure and voice search inaccuracies.

05 — Impact

Competitive Landscape & Strategic Implications

✓

Ella's Competitive Strengths

Quick response time for request execution · Visually appealing and welcoming interface ideal for new users · Entertainment — specifically music and video accuracy using Boomplay · Travel navigation with precise multi-modal route detail · Human-like voice quality · Request-and-response text dialogue box · Strong semantic understanding (7.4 average) and system stability (8.6).

✗

Ella's Critical Weakness Areas

Inability to set calendar events and reminders (only 3.6 average) — a complete functional failure cited by 100% of users. Insufficient knowledge base with outdated information. Limited voice narration for results. Robotic voice quality in some responses. Tonal variation and accent challenges with Kenyan English and Kiswahili. Chat responses disappear quickly from screen.

G

Google Assistant: Why It Leads

Highest comprehensive average (8.1). Superior narration of search results with longer display time. Best reminders and calendar functionality (9.4). Strong texting and communication in English. Sets calendar events by asking follow-up questions ("what time?"). Multilingual search capability and proven accuracy for Kenyan context queries like politicians and current events.

B

Bixby: Niche Strengths & Gaps

Excellent entertainment and travel navigation scores. Strong knowledgebase and collaboration app options. Straight-to-the-point with confirmation prompts before action. However, struggles with Swahili-to-English translation and Kenyan place names (confused Mombasa with Mumbai). Asks which app to use instead of translating directly — a friction point.

05B — Recommendations

Five Strategic Priorities for Ella

01

Fix Calendar & Reminders

The single highest-impact improvement: 100% of users rated competitors better for reminders. Building reliable calendar event creation and alarm-setting functionality is essential before addressing any other gap.

02

Improve Voice Recognition Accuracy

Train Ella to accurately recognize Kenyan English pronunciation, tonal variations, and Kiswahili commands. Users repeatedly cited accent mismatches and word-by-word letter recognition as disruptive to conversational flow.

03

Upgrade Knowledge Base to Real-Time

Users flagged outdated information as a trust-breaker — Ella retrieved incorrect data about Kenya's president. Integrating a real-time data source and improving information precision would directly boost voice search scores.

04

Expand Feature Range & Integrations

Add interoperability with health monitoring devices, smart speakers, and third-party apps. Introduce meeting note-taking and voice cloning features. Keep response text on screen longer before disappearing — currently a usability irritant.

05

Improve Context & Multilingual Support

Add Kiswahili as a fully supported language — including Swahili-to-English translation. Improve context-awareness so Ella can handle multi-request conversations and chain commands without losing context between responses.

06

Leverage Existing Strengths in Marketing

Ella's entertainment, travel navigation, small talk, visual aesthetics, and system stability scores are genuinely competitive. Communicate these differentiators to users — particularly Boomplay music integration and detailed navigation for Kenyan routes.

05C — Conclusion

Ella is competitive —
but reminders and accuracy
are holding it back

The survey revealed that Google Assistant held the highest satisfaction level (6.5 single satisfaction; 8.1 comprehensive average), followed closely by Bixby and Ella. Ella's overall position — competitive across entertainment, travel, small talk, and visual design — demonstrates genuine product strength in the Kenyan market. The top use cases of information, communication, entertainment, travel, and reminders align well with Ella's wins and losses: it performs where users explore, and stumbles where users rely on it most.

The path to becoming the preferred voice assistant in Kenya is achievable. The majority of weaknesses are functional gaps — not fundamental design failures. Users who scored Ella highly did so emphatically, citing quick response times, local cultural awareness (bongo music recognition, Nairobi route precision), and a welcoming personality. Addressing the reminders failure, improving information accuracy, and building Kiswahili support would fundamentally reshape Ella's competitive standing.
